Definitions & Methodology
2-grade target
Each student is expected to advance 2 writing grade levels per school year. A "grade level" = passing the standardized end-of-course test for that level (score 90%+).
Full-year students (S1)
Students who started AlphaWrite in Session 1 (August 2025) and have had the full school year.
Not Spending Enough Time
Student's lifetime average practice volume is below 12.5 XP/day, OR they have been inactive for 14+ school days. 12.5 XP/day is the minimum pace needed to advance 2 grades in a school year.
Stuck in Testing Loop
Student has failed 3+ end-of-course tests at the same grade level without advancing.
Rushes Tests
Student has taken 2+ tests below the minimum time threshold for their grade level (G3-G5: under 25 minutes, G6-G8: under 60 minutes).
Age Grade Below G3
Student is in school Grade 2 or below. AlphaWrite is designed for Grade 3+; younger students may lack the typing/reading readiness for the curriculum.
XP (Experience Points)
A measure of work completed in AlphaWrite. Each activity completed earns XP based on accuracy (80%+ accuracy required to earn full XP).
